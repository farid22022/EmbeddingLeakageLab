# Ethics and data handling

- Prefer synthetic fictional data for development and pilots.
- Do not target real individuals or publish recovered personal information.
- Do not send sensitive reconstructions to external services without explicit
  authorization.
- Keep credentials out of source, manifests, logs, and reports.
- Record data provenance and license/terms before use.
- Keep sensitive/raw data and generated reconstructions local and out of Git.
- Explain misuse risks and limitations in any report.
